OpenMedAgent

Open-Source Autonomous Medical Research & AI Agent Framework

OpenMedAgent is an open-source initiative combining autonomous AI agents, medical literature intelligence, and intelligent workflow automation.

The goal is to build an extensible AI ecosystem that helps healthcare professionals, medical researchers, and developers access scientific knowledge, automate research workflows, and develop specialized AI applications.

🎯 Vision

To make advanced AI research and automation capabilities accessible through transparent, modular, and community-driven open-source software.

🧠 Core Components

1. Medical Research Agent

An AI-powered research assistant designed to:

* Search scientific publications using PubMed.
* Retrieve and organize relevant research papers.
* Summarize scientific literature.
* Compare findings across multiple studies.
* Extract study methodologies and clinical outcomes.
* Generate structured literature reviews.
* Provide traceable scientific references.
* Support evidence-based medical research.

2. Autonomous AI Agent

A modular autonomous agent framework designed to:

* Execute multi-step tasks.
* Perform intelligent task planning.
* Interact with external APIs and tools.
* Process documents and structured information.
* Automate repetitive workflows.
* Coordinate specialized AI agents.
* Support extensible tool integrations.

3. Healthcare AI Applications

Potential applications include:

* Medical literature analysis.
* Clinical guideline retrieval.
* Research methodology assistance.
* Scientific evidence comparison.
* Medical documentation workflows.
* Clinical information organization.

All clinical applications will prioritize human oversight, scientific traceability, privacy, and responsible AI use.

🔬 Responsible AI

OpenMedAgent is intended as a research and productivity platform, not a replacement for qualified medical professionals.

The project aims to prioritize:

* Evidence-based information.
* Verifiable scientific references.
* Patient data privacy.
* Transparency about AI limitations.
* Human verification of clinical outputs.
